# Sales Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data to evaluate **sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, customer purchasing patterns, and business growth opportunities**.

The analysis uses Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Identify sales and revenue trends.
* Find the best-performing products.
* Identify high-performing categories.
* Analyze customer purchasing patterns.
* Evaluate sales contribution across different segments.
* Identify opportunities for improving sales and revenue.
* Provide actionable business recommendations.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses a sales transaction dataset containing information related to:

* Orders
* Products
* Customers
* Sales
* Revenue
* Dates
* Product categories
* Geographic or customer-related attributes

The dataset was provided as part of the **Alfido Tech internship/project assignment**.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Loading & Inspection

* Imported the sales dataset.
* Examined the number of rows and columns.
* Checked data types.
* Identified missing values and duplicate records.
* Examined important sales-related variables.

### 2. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

* Handled missing values.
* Removed duplicate records where required.
* Converted date columns into appropriate formats.
* Created useful calculated fields for sales analysis.
* Prepared the dataset for exploratory analysis.

### 3. Sales Trend Analysis

Analyzed sales performance over time to identify:

* Monthly sales trends
* Revenue fluctuations
* Growth and decline periods
* Seasonal patterns
* Overall sales performance

### 4. Product Performance Analysis

Analyzed products based on:

* Total sales
* Revenue generated
* Number of orders
* Quantity sold
* Product contribution to overall sales

The analysis helped identify the **top-performing products** and products with comparatively lower sales performance.

### 5. Category Performance Analysis

Compared different product categories to determine:

* Highest revenue-generating categories
* Highest-selling categories
* Category contribution to total sales
* Opportunities for category-level growth

### 6. Customer & Sales Analysis

Analyzed customer purchasing behavior to understand:

* High-value customers
* Purchase frequency
* Customer contribution to revenue
* Repeat purchasing behavior

---

## 📈 Key Insights

The analysis identified important patterns in the sales data, including:

* Sales performance varies across different products and categories.
* A smaller group of products contributes significantly to overall revenue.
* Top-performing products and categories can be prioritized for inventory and marketing strategies.
* Sales trends over time help identify periods of higher and lower demand.
* Customer purchasing behavior can be used to identify high-value customer groups.

> Detailed numerical findings and visualizations are available in the project notebook.
